# AfterLife NYC
New York City’s Hart Island is the city’s public cemetery, where people may be buried if they are unclaimed or unidentified, as well as when families choose a public burial.

This project explores patterns behind changes in deaths associated with Hart Island burials. In particular, I want to investigate what is happening across New York City during periods when these deaths increase or decrease. For example, are changes connected to overall death rates, certain causes of death, seasonal patterns, or other population-level trends?

Using historical Hart Island records together with public health data from New York City and federal sources, the project will look for these relationships and test whether they can help estimate future changes. It will also identify periods that are unusually different from historical patterns and explore what may make those periods different.

The goal is not to assume that a particular pattern exists, but to use the data to discover whether meaningful patterns appear and how useful they are for understanding and forecasting changes over time.

This project only studies trends across groups and time periods. It does not attempt to predict whether any individual person will be buried on Hart Island.
